Why the strings must match

The facility choice value and the mentee question’s relevance are built from one canonical facility name per code. If they drifted, Jane would be in the choices but selecting Kerugoya would reveal no one.
Both greens are identical. Selecting Kerugoya will open Jane’s mentee list.
